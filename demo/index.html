Four decisions worth knowing

It loads first, deliberately

Console, fetch, XMLHttpRequest, onerror and unhandled rejections are wrapped at parse time. Put the tag anywhere else and you only capture what happened after it.

Fixed-size ring buffers

200 console lines, 100 requests, 50 errors by default. A page left open all afternoon doesn't grow memory, and the report stays small enough to read.

The UI can't collide with yours

Everything lives in a shadow root, so your CSS can't reach into the panel and the panel can't leak into your page. No reset, no !important war.

No endpoint? Still useful

Without data-endpoint the report is copied to your clipboard as JSON, ready to paste into a chat. The backend is an optimization, not a requirement.
